    📜 LlamaCraft Player Guide: Quests, Bounties & The Dragon Egg Relic¶

    Welcome to the epic adventures of LlamaCraft! Discover 1,000 tiered quests, claim player bounties, and hunt the elusive Dragon Egg in a server-wide King-of-the-Hill event.

    🗺️ Quick Navigation & Quest Commands¶

    Command Action / Purpose
    /quests (or /quest, /q) Open the master Quest Journal to browse and accept missions.
    /quests abort Abandon an active quest you no longer wish to pursue.
    /bounties Open the active Wanted Posters board to view target players.
    /bounty <player> <amount> Place a monetary hit on a rival player ($0\% tax).
    /dragonegg (or /dragon) Check the current status, holder, and dimension of the Dragon Egg.
    /eventhub (or /events) Open the server community events and activities hub.

    🎯 1. The 1,000 Quests Master Engine (/quests)¶

    LlamaCraft features a massive database of 1,000 custom quests organized across 10 gameplay categories. Quests range from beginner progression milestones to endgame trillion-dollar achievements!

    📂 The 10 Quest Domains¶

    Category Typical Objectives Reward Scaling
    Mining & Excavation Mining Deepslate, Ores, and Ancient Debris \$1,000 – \$25 Trillion

    Farming & Agriculture Harvesting wheat, carrots, potatoes, and cocoa \$500 – \$5 Billion
    Deep Sea Fishing Catching specific marine species and size records \$1,000 – \$10 Billion
    Forestry & Timber Chopping forests, gathering saplings \$500 – \$2 Billion
    Livestock & Breeding Breeding sheep, cows, horses, and taming wolves \$500 – \$5 Billion

    Bosses & Endgame Slaying Elder Guardians, Withers, and Ender Dragons \$600M – \$750 Billion
    Absurd & Prestige Extreme grinds, hazard trials, and physics challenges Up to \$20 Quadrillion

    🏆 Milestone Quest Highlights¶

    • Ancient Salvage (Mining Level 43): Mine 128 Ancient Debris $\rightarrow$ Reward: \$1.2 Billion.
    • Abyssal Depths Conqueror (Bosses Level 50): Slay Elder Guardians $\rightarrow$ Reward: \$600 Million.
    • Abyss Cleanser (Mining Level 82): Mine Deepslate $\rightarrow$ Reward: \$25 Trillion.
    • Ancient Debris Eradicator (Absurd Level 89): Mine Ancient Debris in the Nether $\rightarrow$ Reward: \$20 Quadrillion.

    🧪 2. Secret "Absurd" Quests & Environmental Physics¶

    LlamaCraft features custom environmental physics objectives that you won't find in standard Minecraft: * The Item Sacrifice: Destroying diamonds, netherite, or ancient debris in Cacti, Lava, Void, or Fire triggers hidden quest conditions! * Anvil Execution: Dropping an anvil from high above to crush hostile mobs completes specialized combat challenges. * Void Gliding: Flying with an Elytra below $Y=0$ in the End dimension triggers the End Void Glide achievement!

    🤠 3. Player Headhunting & Bounties (/bounties)¶

    Have a rival clan member or a player who stole your loot? Place a public bounty on their head!

    • Placing a Bounty: Run /bounty <player> <amount> (e.g. /bounty Steve 50000).
    • Zero Bounty Tax: Setting bounties carries 0% tax—the entire amount goes directly to whoever claims the kill.
    • The Wanted Board (/bounties): Browse live bounties, target headshots, and reward values.
    • Claiming the Reward: Slay the target in any PvP-enabled zone (Wilderness, /warp pvp) to immediately claim the cash!

    🐉 4. The Server Relic: Dragon Egg Hunt (/dragonegg)¶

    The Dragon Egg is not a static trophy on LlamaCraft—it is an active, persistent server-wide Relic with a permanent bounty on whoever holds it!

                      ✦ THE DRAGON EGG RELIC PROTOCOL ✦
   ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
   │ • Ender Chest Immunity: BLOCKED (Cannot be stashed in /ec)  │
   │ • Container Blacklist:  BLOCKED (No Shulkers, Hoppers, etc) │
   │ • Void Rescue:          ENABLED (Teleports to safe ground)  │
   │ • Global Tracker:       Compass points directly to owner!   │
   └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

    Rules of the Relic:¶

    1. No Hiding in Enderchests: The plugin strictly forbids placing the Dragon Egg inside Ender Chests (/ec).
    2. No Storage Vaults: The Egg cannot be locked inside Shulker Boxes, Hoppers, Droppers, or Dispensers. It must remain in a player's active inventory or placed visibly as a block.
    3. Void Protection: If a carrier jumps into the void, the Egg will NOT be lost—it automatically rescues itself to a safe Y-level coordinate broadcast in chat!
    4. The Dragon Compass: Players can craft a specialized Tracker Compass that locks onto the current Egg holder's location in real-time.
    5. Global Broadcast: Whenever the Egg changes hands, a server-wide announcement notifies all players!
