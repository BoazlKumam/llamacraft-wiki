# 👑 LlamaCraft Player Guide: Ranks, Progression & Player Perks

Level up from humble Stone wanderer to the Godlike Netherite sovereign! Learn how to rank up using in-game cash, expand your claimed territory, claim weekly rank kits, and unlock game-changing perks like fly mode and spawner harvesting.

---

## 🗺️ Quick Navigation & Rank Commands

| Command | Action / Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| `/rankup` (or `/pru`) | Purchase the next server rank if you meet the balance requirement. |
| `/maxrankup` | Automatically purchase as many consecutive ranks as your balance allows. |
| `/ranks` (or `/ranksmenu`) | View the graphical rank ladder, requirements, and unlocked perks. |
| `/kit` (or `/kits`) | Open the weekly kit selector to claim your rank's care package. |
| `/homes` | View all your saved home waypoints. |
| `/sethome <name>` | Save your current location as a named home. |
| `/fly` | Toggle creative flight on or off (**Netherite Rank Only**). |

## 🪜 1. The Official Rankup Ladder

Every player starts at the **Stone** rank for free. Higher ranks are earned purely through in-game gameplay and economy—no pay-to-win real money required!

```
   [Stone] ──($10k)──▶ [Copper] ──($35k)──▶ [Iron]
                                               │
   [Netherite] ◀──($1.0M)── [Diamond] ◀──($300k)── [Gold]
```

### The Progression Matrix

| Rank | Promotion Cost | Chat Prefix | Homes Allowed | Bonus Claim Blocks | Key Unlocked Perks |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Stone** | Free (Start) | `&7[Stone]` | 3 Homes | Base Allowance | Basic survival commands, `/kit stone`, `/kit tools` |
| **Copper** | **\$10,000** | `&6[Copper]` | 4 Homes | **+500 Blocks** | Recipe lookup (`/recipe`), `/kit copper` |
| **Iron** | **\$35,000** | `&f[Iron]` | 5 Homes | **+1,000 Blocks** | **Silk Touch Spawner Mining** (`smartspawner.break`), `/kit iron` |
| **Gold** | **\$100,000** | `&e[Gold]` | 7 Homes | **+2,000 Blocks** | Extended home slots, `/kit gold` |
| **Diamond** | **\$300,000** | `&b[Diamond]` | 10 Homes | **+4,000 Blocks** | Elite enchanted gear kit, 10 home slots, `/kit diamond` |
| **Netherite**| **\$1,000,000** | `&4[Netherite]` | 14 Homes | **+10,000 Blocks**| **CREATIVE FLIGHT (`/fly`)**, God-tier Mending kit, 14 homes |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Total Claim Block Stacking**:
> Progressing from Stone to Netherite awards a cumulative total of **+17,500 Bonus Claim Blocks** directly into your GriefPrevention account!

## 📦 2. Weekly Rank Kits (`/kit`)

Every rank unlocks a dedicated weekly care package with a **7-day cooldown** (604,800 seconds). Higher rank kits come pre-enchanted:

| Kit Name | Rank Required | Weapon & Armor Quality | Pre-Applied Enchantments |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`stone`** | Stone (Default) | Stone Tools + Leather Armor | None |
| **`copper`** | Copper | Full Copper Tools & Armor | None |
| **`iron`** | Iron | Full Iron Tools & Armor | None |
| **`gold`** | Gold | Full Gold Tools & Armor | Sharpness I, Efficiency II |
| **`diamond`** | Diamond | Full Diamond Tools & Armor | Sharpness II, Efficiency III, Protection II, Unbreaking II |
| **`netherite`** | Netherite | Full Netherite Tools & Armor | Sharpness III, Efficiency IV, Protection III, Unbreaking III, **Mending I** |

## 🚀 3. Critical Gameplay Milestones

### 1. Reaching Iron Rank: The Spawner Miner (`smartspawner.break`)
At **Iron Rank**, you unlock permission to break and collect Smart Spawners and natural dungeon spawners with a Silk Touch Pickaxe. If you are still at Stone or Copper rank, spawners will resist extraction!

### 2. Reaching Netherite Rank: Unrestricted Flight (`/fly`)
Netherite is the summit of survival progression on LlamaCraft. Achieving Netherite permanently unlocks `/fly`, enabling seamless base construction, terraforming, and aerial navigation.
